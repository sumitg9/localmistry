From frustration to foundation.

LocalMistry was founded in Mumbai by three engineers who were fed up with unreliable plumbers, no-shows, and hidden charges. We set out to build the platform we wished existed — one that treats both customers and professionals with respect.

In just two years we have grown from a WhatsApp group of 40 technicians to a full-stack marketplace serving 28 cities. Every professional on our platform is background-verified, skill-tested, and rated after every job.

Our obsession is simple: zero surprises for the customer and fair earnings for the expert.
